without directly controlling the physical printer.

through a common interface and device queue.

----- CPU AND DEVICE INDEPENDENCE -----
The CPU can execute other processes while an I/O
device performs its assigned operation.
After I/O completion, the device can notify the
CPU through an interrupt.
This allows CPU and device operations to overlap.
